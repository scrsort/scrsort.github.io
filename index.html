Good to know.

How do I search for text inside a screenshot on Mac?

Click the scrsort icon in the menu bar and type a word. scrsort has already read the text in every screenshot, so matches appear as you type.

Does scrsort upload my screenshots?

No. They stay on your Mac. One is uploaded only when you choose Share for it, and you can stop sharing at any time.

Does it replace the Mac’s own screenshot shortcuts?

No. ⌘⇧3, ⌘⇧4 and ⌘⇧5 keep working, and those screenshots are added automatically. scrsort adds its own shortcuts, which you can change.

Where are my screenshots stored?

In scrsort’s own library in your Application Support folder. The originals in your screenshot folder are never moved or changed.

Which Macs does it work on?

Any Mac running macOS 15 or later.

macOS says it can’t open scrsort. What now?

This early version isn’t notarized by Apple yet, so macOS asks you to confirm it once. Click Done, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway. You only need to do this once per version.

Comfortable with Terminal? Move scrsort to Applications, then paste this line instead. It only affects scrsort.

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Scrsort.app
